Academic Writing Task 1 Blueprints (Qrafiklər, Cədvəllər & Proseslər)

Xətti qrafik, sütunlu diaqram və xəritələr üçün Overview formulaları

Dynamic Chart (Line / Bar over time)

Giriş Paraqrafı (Introduction Formula)
The line graph illustrates changes in [Topic] across [Country/Group] over a [X-year] period between [Year 1] and [Year 2].
Ümumi Baxış (Overview Formula — Band 8.5)
Overall, what stands out from the graph is that [Trend 1 experienced significant upward trajectory], whereas [Trend 2 witnessed a marked decline]. Furthermore, [Category X] consistently maintained the highest figures throughout the timeframe.

Static Comparison (Pie / Table at one point in time)

Giriş Paraqrafı (Introduction Formula)
The pie charts compare the proportion of [Subject] allocated to [Categories] in [Year].
Ümumi Baxış (Overview Formula — Band 8.5)
Overall, it is immediately evident that [Category A] accounted for the vast majority of the total, while [Category B] constituted the least significant proportion.
